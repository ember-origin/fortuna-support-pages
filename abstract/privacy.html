中文

《抽象》（以下简称“本应用”）是一款可离线游玩的单机 iPhone 游戏。本应用尊重您的隐私。

我们收集的信息

当前版本不要求注册账号，不包含广告、分析统计或第三方追踪 SDK。游戏不连接开发者服务器，也不会把您输入的角色名字、游戏进度或其他个人信息发送给开发者。

金币、等级、已解锁内容、当前装扮、角色名字、语言与音效设置仅保存在您的设备本地，以便下次继续游玩。

数据控制与删除

您可在“设置”中修改或清除角色名字；“重置全部进度”会清除等级、金币和解锁进度，但保留角色名字、语言与音效设置。卸载本应用会移除当前设备上的应用数据；系统备份是否留存副本取决于您的 iOS 备份设置。

权限与未来变更

当前版本不使用定位、相机或麦克风录制功能。界面中的扬声器按钮仅控制游戏音效。若未来加入联网、分析、广告或购买功能，我们会先更新本政策和 App Store 隐私信息。

English

Abstract (the “App”) is an offline, single-player iPhone game. The App respects your privacy.

Information We Collect

The current version requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs. It does not connect to a developer server or send character names, game progress, or other personal information to the developer.

Coins, levels, unlocked items, selected outfits, character names, language, and sound preferences are stored only on your device so that you can continue playing.

Your Controls and Deletion

You can edit or clear a character name in Settings. “Reset all progress” clears levels, coins, and unlocks while keeping character names, language, and sound preferences. Uninstalling the App removes its data from the current device; whether a system backup keeps a copy depends on your iOS backup settings.

Permissions and Future Changes

The current version does not use location, camera, or microphone recording features. The speaker button only controls game audio. If networking, analytics, advertising, or purchases are added in the future, this policy and the App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact quickly520@126.com.
